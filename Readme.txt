## 🎮 About the Project

This Power BI dashboard delivers a rich, interactive analysis of **global video game sales**, designed to uncover key trends, top performers, and regional preferences across genres, platforms, and publishers. It combines **advanced visuals** with **data storytelling** to support strategic insights and user-driven exploration.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📈 **Line Chart**: Total sales trend by year  
- 📊 **Horizontal Bar Graphs**:  
  - Most Selling Platforms (PS2)  
  - Most Selling Publishers (Nintendo)  
  - Total Sales by Platform  
  - Total Sales by Publisher  
- 🥧 **Pie Chart**: Sales distribution by genre and region  
- 🗺️ **Filled Map**: Most popular genre by region  
- 🧩 **Decomposition Tree**: Total Sales → Year → Region → Genre  
- 💬 **Q&A Visual**: Natural language queries for instant insights  
- 🎞️ **Intro & Thank You Slides**: Framing the dashboard with professional polish

---

## 📂 Dataset Overview

- **Source**: Public dataset on global video game sales  
- **Fields Used**: Year, Genre, Platform, Publisher, Region, Global Sales  
- **Data Volume**: Thousands of records spanning multiple decades  
- **Cleaning & Transformation**: Performed in Power BI using Power Query and DAX

---

## 🔍 Insights and Recommendations

- 📈 **Sales peaked between 2008–2009**, marking the industry's golden years  
- 🕹️ **Action genre** recorded the highest global sales  
- 🌎 **North America** emerged as the top-performing region  
- 🏆 **PS2** was the most selling platform, while **Nintendo** led among publishers  
- 📌 **Recommendation**: Focus on genre-platform-region alignment to maximize market impact

## 📌 How to Use

1. **Open the dashboard in Power BI Desktop**  
2. **Navigate through slides** using the navigation pane  
3. **Interact with visuals** via filters, slicers, and Q&A  
4. **Explore decomposition paths** to drill into specific sales breakdowns  
5. **Use insights** for market analysis, business strategy, or portfolio review
