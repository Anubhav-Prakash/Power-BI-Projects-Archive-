# Flipkart Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview

This project delivers a **multi-page interactive dashboard** built in Microsoft Power BI to analyze Flipkart’s sales performance, customer behavior, payment preferences, and regional distribution. The solution is designed to provide **actionable insights** for decision-making in inventory management, marketing, pricing, and customer service.

The dashboard demonstrates advanced Power BI capabilities, including **page navigation, dynamic slicers, diagnostic visuals, and natural language Q&A integration**.

---

## Objectives

- Track sales performance over time across product categories and regions.  

- Identify seasonal trends and campaign-driven fluctuations.  

- Analyze customer satisfaction through ratings and its relationship with sales.  

- Assess payment method preferences and their impact on sales volumes.  

- Visualize regional sales distribution to highlight demand hotspots and growth opportunities.  

---

## Core Features

- **Page Navigation**: Custom buttons for seamless movement across multiple report pages.  

- **Dynamic Slicers**: Filters for Product Categories, Units Sold, Region, and Payment Method.  

- **Advanced Visuals**:  

  - *Key Influencers*: Identifies drivers of sales and customer satisfaction.  

  - *Line & Clustered Column Chart*: Tracks sales trends and comparative performance.  

  - *Q&A Visual*: Enables natural language queries for ad-hoc analysis.  

  - *Map Visual*: Displays geographic sales distribution.  

- **Interactive Storytelling**: Modular pages designed for clarity and recruiter-ready presentation.  

---

## Dataset

The dashboard leverages a structured dataset with the following fields:

- Sales Amount  

- Quantity Sold  

- Discount (%)  

- Customer Rating  

- Region  

- Product Category  

- Payment Method  

- Order Date / Order ID
